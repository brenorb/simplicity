Paper 2022/1491

LMS-SM3 and HSS-SM3: Instantiating Hash-based Post-Quantum Signature Schemes with SM3

Siwei Sun, School of Cryptology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China
Tianyu Liu, School of Cryptology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China
Zhi Guan, Peking University, China
Yifei He, Peking University, China
Jiwu Jing, School of Cryptology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China
Lei Hu, School of Cryptology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China
Zhenfeng Zhang, Trusted Computing and Information Assurance Laboratory, Institute of Software, China
Hailun Yan, School of Cryptology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China
Abstract

We instantiate the hash-based post-quantum stateful signature schemes LMS and HSS described in RFC 8554 and NIST SP 800-208 with SM3, and report on the results of the preliminary performance test.
