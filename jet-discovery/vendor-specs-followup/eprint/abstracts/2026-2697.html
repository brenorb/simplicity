Paper 2026/2697

Batched Threshold Predicate Encryption from Succinct LWE

Shayeef Murshid, Indian Statistical Institute Kolkata
Ramprasad Sarkar, Indian Institute of Information Technology Lucknow
Mriganka Mandal, Indian Statistical Institute Kolkata
Abstract

Predicate encryption extends attribute-based encryption by providing fine-grained access control while hiding the attribute associated with a ciphertext beyond what the secret keys held by an adversary necessarily reveal. Batched identity-based encryption enables authorization with respect to a designated collection of identities. We introduce batched threshold predicate encryption (BT-PE), which combines batch-scoped authorization, expressive predicate-based access control, and threshold decryption. We give a lattice-based construction supporting polynomial-size arithmetic-circuit predicates, compact batch digests, and compact per-party key shares. Decryption is possible only when the encrypted attribute belongs to the authorized batch, satisfies the required predicate, and a sufficiently large set of parties participates. We prove correctness and query-selective one-sided security under the $\ell$-succinct LWE assumption, together with standard assumptions on digital signatures and compute-and-compare obfuscation.
