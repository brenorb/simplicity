Paper 2026/2554

Concrete LDVR Attacks with Fewer Signers

Renas Bacho, Ruhr University Bochum
Abstract

We improve the concrete parameters of the attacks on the low-dimensional vector representation (LDVR) problem by Rodríguez García and Tessaro (ePrint 2026/2126), whose attack on canonical evaluation points uses $5{,}020$ signers. By combining their Chinese remainder theorem construction with a generalized birthday search over many challenges, we obtain a concrete attack with $488$ signers and $173$ corruptions over the Curve25519 scalar field. In $64$ batches of $2^{25}$ challenges, the search finds solutions for six distinct challenges. We compare the counts measured at each stage of the search with predictions from an explicit heuristic model. For adversarially chosen evaluation points, we build on the binary attacks of Rodríguez García and Tessaro and give a construction that attains the optimal LDVR success probability for every oracle query budget when $2\le n\le253$ and $t_c=t$ in the uniform oracle model. At $228$ signers and $114$ corruptions, our implementation finds a successful subset using SHA-512 challenges after a cumulative total of $360.13$ million queries across two runs, taking $168.60$ seconds in total. We also give an explicit set of $256$ evaluation points for which an efficient subset search attack using $128$ corruptions succeeds with probability at least $51.92\%$ per uniform challenge. For both canonical and adversarially chosen evaluation points, we analyze the query complexity and report measured and estimated running times.
