Price limits are strictly inside the endpoints, so the only crossable tick is zero. The predicate computes the exact source first step, identifies actual crossing (even if price limit equals tick0), switches to the other committed liquidity and computes a second step only if input/output remains and the limit is not reached. A zero-liquidity interval can be traversed without creating a payment or fee. Both input/output sizing and both directions use source amount/price/fee rounding. Actual spent amount may be less than its specified maximum at a limit, with complete native custody and optional input/output bounds.

The source fee-growth-increment floor is `fee * 2^128 / activeLiquidity`. Each range stores the growth accumulated since its position's last LP update. In this map, a fee charged in a band increases only that band's source inside growth; crossing flips the source outside representation without changing either inside history. Outer ticks are never crossed, and any fixed initialization offset cancels when taking the source position's growth difference. With one position per range, the native counter therefore stores that difference directly. At a position update, compute `floor(deltaGrowth * oldLiquidity / 2^128)` once, add to owed, and reset the delta. Clearing and reinitializing an empty position has zero old liquidity and starts a new delta. The native persisted delta must fit128 bits; the numerator product remains full width. No claim of unrestricted source counter wrapping is made.

Burning rounds down, reduces that position's liquidity and records owed principal until collection; a zero burn checkpoints an existing position's fees. Collect only pays existing owed balances and does not checkpoint uncrystallized fees. The actual pool balances include outstanding owed claims; owed and accrued fees cannot also be counted as separate issued assets. The initialized lineage starts with no unbacked LP, and complete mint/burn/swap/collect rules carry its reserves and ownership forward.
