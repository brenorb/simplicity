# CryptoGrowth deployment and independence audit

This is the complete bounded audit of the **20 scopes below**, dated 2026-11-05. The audit itself adds **zero registrations or corpus-count credit**; implementation evidence is recorded explicitly per row and in the linked validation report. This is a source/deployment plan, not a cost measurement or a full-text review of the eprint archive.

WebAuthn now has a complete native wallet implementation with **17/17 full vectors PASS and actual exit0**, including canonical composition CMR and prepared-path checks. Its current qualification and resource evidence is in [CRYPTO-FAMILIES.md](CRYPTO-FAMILIES.md); shared corpus registration/cost analysis remains separate. FairSwap's full-source gate and native scope are solved, its23vector implementation compiles, and full native verification remains pending. Cashu requires a distinct native redemption/backing design and an explicit privacy tradeoff. The ten distinct full-verifier families and the cross-curve swap are substantial unfinished work. Four other rows are upgrades to existing groups; two have no established new onchain predicate.

The arithmetic upper cap is fourteen possible new groups **if every gated scope becomes a complete, independently validated program**. This is neither a forecast nor evidence that fourteen near-term new applications exist. In particular, implementing a shared kernel or adding a signature/payout label does not earn that credit.

## Source coverage and reproduction

The separate [source packet](../vendor-specs/crypto-growth/MANIFEST.md) records all 29 retrieval attempts: **20 valid retrieved documents and 9 access gaps**. Eight direct eprint PDFs returned403; the legacy FairSwap author-dissertation endpoint returned a200 bot challenge rather than a PDF. Its exact university bitstream later yielded the genuine complete PDF. Both attempts remain recorded; the bot response is stored as HTML and is not counted as a retrieved paper. A successful HTTP status alone is insufficient.

Complete existing BIP340/RFC9591/VT-Schnorr sources are reused from their original vendor directories and manifests. Primary metadata is adequate to retain a research lead, not to claim a complete protocol implementation or exclusion. The published WabiSabi PDF is a separate2021-08-25 revision with its own explicit CC BY4.0 notice; it is not silently substituted for the inaccessible current eprint revision.

All external material is data outside the Haskell build. No downloaded code is executed. The Cashu source is MIT; the Babylon description is retained with its actual Business Source License1.1 text. RFC/IETF, W3C and PDF notices are recorded separately, without inferring a blanket software license.

`python3 tools/crypto_growth_report.py` verifies packet hashes and renders every row offline. `python3 tools/fetch_crypto_growth_sources.py` intentionally refreshes the fixed documentary URLs and records any access failures; it should not be used merely to reproduce this snapshot.

This audit itself adds no Cabal registration, CMR, WU or candidate-savings claim. The current source coverage inventory remains separate. WebAuthn's completed17vector validation and the separately approved crypto component families have their own frozen evidence in CRYPTO-FAMILIES.md. The other scopes do not inherit that success. Every implementation needs a single-entry witness dataflow audit, strict encoding/range checks, independent positive/adversarial vectors and the fresh main cost run.
