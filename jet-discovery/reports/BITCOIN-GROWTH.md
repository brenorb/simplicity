# Bitcoin application growth: scope and evidence

This batch adds three registered, distinct bounded spending conditions from primary Bitcoin specifications. It does not count a protocol name as an implementation. No cost or full-corpus result is claimed here; complete standalone same-CMR execution evidence is recorded separately from the still-pending shared run. The remaining research scopes below are unregistered and receive no credit.

The first standalone runs used the harness's simplified funding-script placeholders. A subsequent fixture hardening constructs each real native funding script as `TapTweak(InternalKey, TapLeaf(CMR))`, with no sibling path. The predicates and their CMRs are unchanged. The fresh complete27-vector run with correctly committed funding outputs **passed all27 cases**, with every vector retaining its application's CMR. Log: `reports/data/bitcoin-growth-batch1-rts.txt`; exact five-module source copies and SHA256 records: `bitcoin-growth-batch1-provenance.json`. At `-N1 -M3G`, elapsed time was1336.692 seconds, max live1,035,642,480 bytes and total RTS memory2345 MiB. These are actual jetted Haskell Simplicity evaluations, not a Bitcoin Core/C interpreter or a serialized network-transaction consensus test.

The duplicate-index EVICT attack has otherwise valid promise signatures and a fresh remaining signature under the *duplicate-subtraction* key, so its rejection exercises uniqueness independently. Covenant-negative transactions are re-signed where necessary. The SPV receipt forces the native payout to the actual authenticated historical recipient: a witness-supplied txid or payment amount is never accepted without reconstructing and authenticating the complete supported transaction.
